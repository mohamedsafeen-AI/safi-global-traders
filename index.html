SAFI Global Traders is a newly established agricultural import-export business with a vision to connect quality Indian agricultural products with international markets. We are building our business step by step with a strong focus on quality, transparency, reliability, and responsible trade.

Our History & Vision

SAFI Global Traders was founded with a vision to build a reliable agricultural import-export business connecting Indian agricultural products with international markets. Our name — SAFI, meaning "pure" — reflects the promise we aim to deliver with every order: honest, quality goods.

As a growing business, we are focused on developing strong supplier relationships, maintaining product quality, understanding customer requirements, and building long-term trust through responsible trade.

Our Services

Global agricultural supply, sourcing, quality control, custom packaging, and export logistics for farm goods and perishables.

What We Do

Agricultural Export Services

End-to-end support for importers and buyers, from farm-gate sourcing and inspection to packing, documentation, and delivery.

Agricultural Sourcing & Procurement

We identify growers, mills, and packhouses for rice, pulses, spices, vegetables, and fruits, then negotiate specifications, volumes, and harvest windows on your behalf.

Quality Assurance & Inspection

Pre-shipment inspection, moisture and grading checks, residue and aflatoxin coordination, and lab testing support so every lot matches the agreed specification.

Custom Packaging & Private Labeling

Retail-ready and bulk packing in buyer-specified sizes, from jute and PP bags to ventilated crates, with your own branding, labels, and barcode formats.

Export Logistics & Global Shipping

Freight booking, reefer and dry container coordination, phytosanitary and certificate documentation, and freight-foreworded delivery to your destination port.
